Accounting

  • ACCT 415x Intermediate Financial Accounting for Non-Accounting Majors

    Units: 4
    Terms Offered: FaSp
    In-depth study of balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow statement issued from the perspective of a user (not preparer) of corporate financial reports. Not open to accounting majors.
    Prerequisite: ACCT 410x  or BUAD 280  or BUAD 305 
    Registration Restriction: Not open to accounting majors
    Credit Restriction: Not available for degree credit to accounting majors
    Duplicates Credit In former ACCT 411x and ACCT 412x
    Instruction Mode: Lecture
    Grading Option: Letter

    Units: 4
    Terms Offered: FaSp
    Analysis of corporate financial reports from a decision-maker’s perspective.
    Prerequisite: (BUAD 215  or BUAD 306  or BUAD 308 ) and (BUAD 280  or BUAD 305  or ACCT 410 )
    Recommended Preparation: ACCT 370  or ACCT 377

    Grading Option: Letter
